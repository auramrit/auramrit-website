The Auramrit Difference

Why Ordinary Supermarket Dry Fruits Feel Lifeless

Nuts and seeds are living superfoods rich in delicate Omega-3 fats. When exposed to heat and warehouse dust for months, their oils turn rancid. Here is how Auramrit preserves nature's true nectar:

12°C Cold-Chain Storage

Unlike wholesale mandis where nuts sit in 40°C summer heat, our harvest is stored in climate-controlled 12°C vaults right until the hour your order is packed.

Zero Acid Bleaching or Talc

Many commercial pistachios and cashews are whitened using sulphur dioxide. Auramrit nuts retain their natural earthy ivory hue and 100% raw micronutrient profile.

Batch-Wise Harvest QR Code

Every Auramrit pouch features a scannable QR code revealing the exact orchard origin, date of shelling, moisture percentage, and aflatoxin lab safety report.
